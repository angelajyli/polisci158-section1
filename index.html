About

I'm a junior at Stanford studying Economics and Data Science. I'm interested in the physical systems behind the AI economy: the supply chains that produce chips, the energy needed to power data centers, and the infrastructure that makes large-scale buildout possible.

Research Interests & Project Ideas

Semiconductor & AI Supply Chains

How chips and their key inputs move across borders, and how export controls reshape the AI industry.

View project idea
Semiconductor supply chain tracker An agent that scrapes trade data, company filings, and export-control announcements to map where chips and key inputs (like lithography tools and rare materials) are made, and to flag how new policies shift those flows.

Energy Demand & Data Centers

What AI's growing power demand means for data center buildout and the electric grid.

View project idea
Data center energy monitor An agent that collects utility filings, grid interconnection queues, and local news on proposed data centers, then estimates their expected power demand and which regions face the most grid strain.

Infrastructure Development

The economic and political constraints on building fabs, data centers, and transmission lines.

View project idea
Infrastructure permitting and politics dashboard An agent that follows permitting decisions, zoning hearings, and state and local legislation on data centers, fabs, and transmission lines, to study where public opposition or policy slows AI infrastructure buildout.
